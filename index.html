About

Engineering with a business mindset.

I work across the whole development lifecycle, turning business requirements into applications that hold up in production. Most of that work lives in web APIs, database-driven systems, payment gateway integrations, and accounting software connectivity, with cloud tooling around the edges.

Billing is a domain where small errors are expensive and visible. That shapes how I build: careful data modelling, explicit reconciliation, and integrations that fail loudly rather than quietly. I value clear communication and collaborative delivery over heroics.

Alongside my development career I manage a small e-commerce business. It gives me a hands-on view of operations, customer experience, and the practical cost of a system that doesn't quite work — perspective I bring back into the products I build.
